## Free Software and Tools

These are free tools from other people, shared here as resources.

- [SVGnest](https://github.com/Jack000/SVGnest) - Source: Jack Qiao on GitHub. A free tool that arranges parts on a sheet to waste less material.
- [Deepnest (deepnest-next)](https://github.com/deepnest-next/deepnest) - Source: the deepnest-next community on GitHub. A free desktop program for nesting parts for laser cutting.
- [LibreCAD](https://github.com/LibreCAD/LibreCAD) - Source: the LibreCAD project on GitHub. A free 2D drawing program that opens and saves DXF files.
- [DXF Viewer example](https://vagran.github.io/dxf-viewer-example/) - Source: vagran on GitHub. A free in-browser viewer for opening and looking at DXF files.

## Guides and Charts

These are free articles and charts from other sources, shared here as links.

- [Sheet Metal Gauge Chart](https://www.steelnow.com/blog/sheet-metal-gauge-chart) - Source: SteelNow. Shows how thick each gauge number really is for steel, stainless, and aluminum.
- [Laser cutting basics: The science of burr-free laser cutting](https://www.thefabricator.com/thefabricator/article/lasercutting/laser-cutting-basics-the-science-of-burr-free-laser-cutting) - Source: The Fabricator. Explains why rough edges form on laser cut parts and how to get cleaner cuts.
- [Gas gives the big assist in laser cutting](https://www.thefabricator.com/thefabricator/article/lasercutting/gas-gives-the-big-assist-in-laser-cutting) - Source: The Fabricator. Explains what assist gases like nitrogen and oxygen do during a cut.
