# Helpful Links

Free resources I find useful for laser cutting, nesting and shop safety. All content belongs to the original sources credited below; these are just links.

## Machines and Automation

- [AS LUL 3015 Automatic Storage with Load and Unload Functions (PDF brochure)](https://www.amada.eu/fileadmin/Repository/Products/Brochures/ASLUL_3015_2pages_2021_EN.pdf) - Source: AMADA EUROPE. Overview of the AS-LUL tower that loads raw sheets and unloads finished sheets.

## Nesting

- [Nesting fundamentals for laser cutting stability](https://www.thefabricator.com/thefabricator/article/lasercutting/nesting-fundamentals-for-laser-cutting-stability) - Source: The Fabricator, by Tim Heston. Tips on laying out parts so they stay stable during cutting.

## Safety

- [Laser Hazards - Overview](https://www.osha.gov/laser-hazards) - Source: U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). How lasers can affect eyes and skin, and where to find official guidance.
- [Laser Safety Standards](https://www.lia.org/resources/laser-safety-information/laser-safety-standards) - Source: The Laser Institute (LIA). Overview of the ANSI Z136 laser safety standards.
